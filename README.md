## 3. Project Overview

BrightSpark Electrical Services is a local residential and commercial
electrical contracting business. The business was founded in 2021 by Mr
Gideon Mpasa and started in the small village of Qwabi.

The website was developed to give BrightSpark a professional online
presence and make it easier for customers to find information about the
company and its services.

The website consists of five linked pages:

-   **Home (`index.html`)** -- introduces BrightSpark and provides quick
    links to services.
-   **About (`about.html`)** -- provides company history, mission,
    vision, future plans and target customers.
-   **Services (`services.html`)** -- displays electrical services and
    estimated starting prices.
-   **Enquiry (`enquiry.html`)** -- contains a service enquiry form.
-   **Contact (`contact.html`)** -- provides contact information,
    service locations and a contact form.

A shared **`style.css`** file controls the design across all pages.

## 4. Website Goals and Objectives

The main goals and objectives are:

1.  Create a professional online presence for BrightSpark Electrical
    Services.
2.  Provide clear information about the company's electrical services.
3.  Display estimated service starting prices.
4.  Make it easy for customers to submit service enquiries.
5.  Provide contact information and service locations.
6.  Improve the customer experience through simple navigation and
    readable information.
7.  Make the website responsive on desktop, tablet and mobile devices.
8.  Support the company's future expansion and planned nationwide
    branches.
